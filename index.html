Our Story & Safety Standards

A Safe, Joyful Place for Little Hearts to Bloom

Little Hearts Learning Daycare was created with one clear belief: children thrive when they feel safe, loved, encouraged, and understood.

Thoughtful Home Care with Early Learning

Our Lemoore home daycare blends structured early learning, outdoor exploration, early literacy, creative arts, and a warm family atmosphere. Because we maintain a low child-to-provider ratio, every child receives patient, individualized attention tailored to their developmental stage.

Licensing & Caregiver Credentials

California Family Child Care: Operating in compliance with California Family Child Care home standards (full state license issuance upon final approval). All required health, safety, and background clearances are active today:

  • ✓CPR & Pediatric First Aid Certified: Current certification for infant, toddler, and child emergencies.
  • ✓Background-Checked Caregiver: Cleared criminal record and child abuse index checks.
  • ✓Low Child-to-Provider Ratio: Small group sizes for closer supervision and calm routines.
  • ✓Nutritious Meals & Snacks Included: Fresh breakfast, lunch, and afternoon snack served daily.

What Parents Can Expect Every Day

  • 💛Warm Morning Welcome: Calm, unhurried drop-offs so children transition smoothly into their day.
  • 📱Daily Parent Updates: Clear communication on meals, naps, milestones, and playful moments.
  • 🧼Clean, Child-Safe Spaces: Daily sanitization of toys, learning stations, and rest areas.
  • 📚Play-Based Curriculum: Age-appropriate songs, storytime, motor skill activities, and school readiness.

Meet Your Provider

Guided by Experience, Patience & Heart

“Every parent deserves to focus on their day knowing their child is safe, loved, and growing.”

Mina’s journey with children began in her home country of Indonesia, where she spent over a decade working with young learners in international schools and English language programs. Her passion for early childhood education led her to establish her own learning center—nurturing curious minds and building confident communicators from the ground up.

After relocating to the United States and making Lemoore home, Mina founded Little Hearts Learning Daycare with one mission: to create a safe, joyful space where every child feels celebrated and every family feels right at home.

Predictable & Flexible

A Typical Day at Little Hearts

Consistent routines help children feel secure, while infant naps and feedings always follow each baby’s individual schedule.

6:00 AM – 8:00 AM

Warm Arrival & Quiet Free Play

Gentle welcome, puzzles, books, and soft morning activities as friends arrive.

8:00 AM – 8:45 AM

Nutritious Breakfast & Handwashing

Family-style breakfast served together with healthy morning habits.

8:45 AM – 10:00 AM

Circle Time, Story & Learning Stations

Songs, calendar, early literacy, sensory bins, and age-grouped developmental play.

10:00 AM – 11:30 AM

Outdoor Exploration & Garden Play

Fresh air at the Chalk Board, Bubble Station, and Little Hearts Garden.

11:30 AM – 12:30 PM

Balanced Lunch & Wind-Down

Homemade, kid-friendly lunch followed by cleanup and calm story reading.

12:30 PM – 2:30 PM

Nap & Quiet Rest Time

Cozy rest period for toddlers and preschoolers; quiet reading/homework help for older kids.

2:30 PM – 4:30 PM

Afternoon Snack, Crafts & Creative Play

Healthy afternoon snack, art projects, building blocks, and outdoor play (weather permitting).

4:30 PM – 6:00 PM

Tabletop Games & Family Pick-Up

Relaxed play stations and daily parent check-ins at pick-up.

Clear Expectations

Parent Policies & House Guidelines

Simple, respectful guidelines keep every child healthy, safe, and happy.

🕕 Drop-Off & Pick-Up

Hours are 6:00 AM–6:00 PM Monday–Friday. Please notify Mina if you will be running late or if an authorized alternate person is picking up.

🤒 Health & Wellness

To protect all children, children must be fever-free and symptom-free for at least 24 hours without medication before returning to care.

🎒 What to Bring

Please label a change of weather-appropriate clothes, a comfort item for nap time, and diapers/wipes/formula if applicable.
